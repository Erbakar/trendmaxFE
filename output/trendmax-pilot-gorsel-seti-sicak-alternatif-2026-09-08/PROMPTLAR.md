# Sıcak Alternatif Düzenleme Promptu

Her görsel, ilk pilot setindeki karşılığını düzenleme hedefi olarak kullanmıştır.

## Ortak prompt

Use case: lighting-weather

Create a warm-color alternative for the supplied Trendmax asset. Change only the overall lighting and color atmosphere from cool blue to a refined warm premium retail palette. Use bright warm late-morning studio daylight, soft golden illumination and gentle natural shadows. The result should feel premium, inviting and trustworthy, never dark or orange-heavy.

Color palette: clean warm ivory, cream, pale sand, light natural oak, champagne metal and very restrained muted copper accents. Keep product blacks, grays, whites and metallic materials realistic and color-neutral. Screen imagery may retain restrained natural blue for visual contrast.

Preserve the input composition, aspect ratio, product placement, negative-space zones, perspective and crop. Do not add, remove, replace, duplicate or reshape any object. Keep every product unchanged except for natural warmer light reflections. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand names, no watermark, no people. Avoid yellow cast, sepia, excessive orange, dark brown and oversaturation.
